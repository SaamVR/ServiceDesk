## Invariants to independently verify
1. **Human approval:** AI suggestion cannot send. Cancellation aborts only browser wait, not provider billing. Timeout of an approval is *indeterminate* and should not silently mint a new idempotency key; customer delivery must be read back.
2. **HTTP reply truth:** Successful receipt must bind exact conversationId/sourceMessageId to request, include a well-formed outbox/message/workspace ID, and not expose extra private fields. `QUEUED_FOR_PROVIDER` must not report DELIVERED. It cannot independently prove the workspace belongs to the current session; that remains the authorized facade/RLS responsibility.
3. **Replay truth:** Returning EXISTING_REPLY with persisted DELIVERED is valid, provided it is authorized and source-scoped. No downstream callback stub proves real delivery or the existence of a DB event.
4. **Release truth:** Require same exact combined-head controller SHA for CI, Chromium synthetic/browser proof and PG17. Require exact deployed review source SHA plus authenticated real tenant journey separately. Never substitute source-only test for the latter.
